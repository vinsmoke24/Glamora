Trigger: Appointment marked as “Completed”
→ Fetch all booked services
→ Sum prices at time of booking (AppointmentServices.PriceAtBooking)
→ Apply package/discounts if any
→ Apply GST (if enabled)
→ Generate invoice and mark appointment as “Billed”
 




#Code Sample

decimal subtotal = appointment.Services.Sum(s => s.PriceAtBooking);
decimal discount = CalculateDiscount(appointment.CustomerId, services);
decimal tax = ApplyGST(subtotal - discount);
decimal total = subtotal - discount + tax;

CreateInvoice(appointmentId, subtotal, discount, tax, total);




Token Payment Handling

- During booking: collect advance token (e.g., ₹100)
- Store as `Payment` record (linked to Appointment, not Invoice yet)
- When full invoice is generated:
    → TotalAmount = Total - TokenPaid
    → Token is applied in final bill
