=== Reporting Engine

==== 1. Daily Sales Summary
- Total revenue by:
  - Appointments
  - Product sales
  - Payment modes (cash, UPI, etc.)
- Total discounts given
- Taxes collected
- Cash in hand (reconciled with DailyCashLogs)

==== 2. Appointment Report
- Appointments by:
  - Status (completed, cancelled, no-show)
  - Service type
  - Staff member
  - Time slots (peak hours)

==== 3. Staff Performance Report
- Number of services handled
- Revenue generated per staff
- Working hours vs. appointment hours
- Customer ratings (if feedback added later)

==== 4. Inventory Usage Report
- Product usage by date range
- Top consumed products
- Low stock alerts
- Products nearing expiry (if applicable)

==== 5. Customer Report
- Most frequent customers
- Total spend per customer
- Services used over time

⚙️ Technical Implementation Notes
Reports generated via SQL views + stored procedures

Exposed through ASP.NET API endpoints

UI shows filters: date range, branch, staff, category

Export options: CSV / Excel / Print

🧠 Optimization Ideas (Phase 2)
Schedule auto-email of daily/weekly reports

BI Dashboard (PowerBI embedded or Chart.js)

Notification triggers: low sales today, low stock, etc.
